Rooted in tradition, refined for today

MY.CAFÉ was created to honour the authentic slow-drip rituals of South Indian filter decoction, marrying traditional warmth with artisanal pastry and sourdough craft.

Every morning begins before sunrise — slow decoctions steeping drop by drop, and sourdough loaves baking until golden brown and crackling. We started with twelve seats and one grinder in 2019. Today we roast our own beans, bake our own bread and still know most of our guests by name.
