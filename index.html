The ritual

From bean to cup

01

Source

We buy directly from four shade-grown estates in Coorg, Chikmagalur, Araku and Wayanad — paying above fair-trade price for the best micro-lots of the harvest.

  • Single estate
  • Shade grown
  • Hand picked
02

Roast

Small 5 kg batches on our in-house roaster, profiled by hand and cupped every morning so each lot shows off its own character instead of tasting “roasty”.

  • Micro-batch
  • Profiled by hand
  • Cupped daily
03

Brew

Gravity-filtered decoction drips through brass for hours; our cold brew steeps for 24. Precise temperature, precise water, and plenty of patience.

  • Slow drip
  • 93°C water
  • 24hr cold steep
04

Bake

The ovens fire up at 4 AM. Sourdough from a six-year-old starter, croissants laminated over three days, and cardamom buns that rarely last past ten.

  • Wild yeast
  • Hand laminated
  • Baked at 4 AM
05

Serve

Finally, a cup placed in front of you — unhurried, at the right temperature, with a story about where it came from if you want one. Stay as long as you like.

  • Unhurried
  • Your name on the cup
  • Free refills of water

Our philosophy

Rooted in tradition, refined for today

MY.CAFÉ was created to honour the slow-drip rituals of South Indian filter decoction — and to marry that warmth with artisanal pastry and sourdough craft.

Every morning begins before sunrise: decoctions steeping drop by drop, loaves baking until golden and crackling. No rush. No shortcuts. Just good things made slowly.
